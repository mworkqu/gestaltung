# Privacy policy

This policy explains what CAD Champions collects about you, why we collect it, who sees it and how you can ask us to delete it. CAD Champions is a training program run by [TODO-MO: company legal name], which also runs Gestaltung360.

> Effective date: [TODO-MO: effective date]. This is a draft and has not been approved yet.

## 1. What we collect and why

**Your account.** Your name, email address and phone number, the school or university you study at, your study level and your password (we store only a protected version of it, never the password itself). We use these to create your account, sign you in and contact you about your training and tests.

**Date of birth.** We ask for it to know if you are under 18. If you are, we need your guardian's permission (see section 3).

**Guardian details (under 18 only).** Your guardian's name and phone number, and a tick to confirm that they agree to you taking part.

**Your training choices.** The CAD software you train with (Inventor, Fusion or SolidWorks), your experience level and whether you have access to a Windows PC. We use them to show you the right lessons and to prepare your tests.

**Your learning and test activity.** Your Get Ready progress, training progress, test attempts, timings, answers, scores and feedback. We use them to run the test, grade it and show you your results.

**Your uploads.** The files you upload for a test, such as drawings (PDF), model files and ZIP packages. We use them to check and grade your work. How long we keep them is in section 6.

**Payment proofs and payment records.** If you pay by Fawran, you upload a picture of your transfer receipt. An admin looks at it to confirm your payment. We also keep a record of each payment: amount, method, status and reference code.

**Usage information.** Basic information about how the site is used, such as which pages are visited and which actions are taken, and technical logs needed to keep the service secure and working. [TODO-MO: confirm the analytics tools in use]

**Messages to us.** What you write when you contact support or use the Need help button.

If you are under 18 when you sign up, we ask for your guardian's name and phone number and for their agreement that you may take part. We may contact your guardian about your account, payments or safety. If you are a guardian and want to see, correct or delete a child's information, or to withdraw your agreement, contact us (section 8).

## 4. AI-assisted drawing checks

When you submit a drawing for a test, an AI model makes a first pass: it looks at your drawing and compares it with the checklist for that task. A trainer then confirms or corrects the result. The AI result is a first check only. Your final score is confirmed by a person.

To do this, the drawing (a PDF) and the task checklist are sent securely to a third-party AI provider. ## 5. Payments

You can pay by Fawran bank transfer today. You send the transfer yourself from your bank app and then upload the proof. An admin checks it and approves your payment.

Card payments may be added later through a payment provider called SkipCash. If that happens, you will enter your card details on the provider's own secure page. We never see or store your card number.
